The Toolkit

Every Tool,
One Place

Five free tools built for self-taught guitarists. No sign-up, no cost, no ads. They all run right in your browser.

Practice Tools

The Full Lineup

Everything you need for a practice session — get in tune, stay in time, look up a chord, and write down what you figured out.

Chord Library

Every chord across all 12 keys — open positions and barre shapes, with an instant diagram for each voicing. Your complete reference when you need a shape fast.

  • All 12 keys
  • 3 voicings each
  • Diagrams
Open Tool

Chord Identifier

Click any fret position on the interactive fretboard and instantly find out what chord you're holding. Export the diagram as SVG or PNG when you want to keep it.

  • Interactive fretboard
  • Names any shape
  • SVG / PNG export
Open Tool

Metronome

A full-featured metronome to keep your timing tight. Adjustable BPM, tap tempo, time signatures, subdivisions, and accent control for every practice session.

  • Tap tempo
  • Time signatures
  • Subdivisions
Open Tool

Tuner

Get perfectly in tune before you play a note. Uses your device microphone to detect pitch in real time — no extra app and no clip-on tuner needed.

  • Real-time pitch
  • Uses your mic
  • No app needed
Open Tool

Tab Creator

Write guitar tabs right in your browser. Add hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends and vibrato, then print your tab or download it as a PNG to keep.

  • Full articulations
  • Print ready
  • PNG download
